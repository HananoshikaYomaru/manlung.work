About

Bridging learning goals and rigorous systems.

I am a software engineer and content creator focused on learning technology. At TWU I work as a Learning Technology Specialist and Moodle administrator; before that I was a software engineer and team lead at a Hong Kong startup.

Most of what I know is self-taught. I care about making learning easier to start and sustain—clear resources, workflows that fit real courses, and platforms that stay reliable once courses are well underway. My work bridges those teaching goals with system reality: LMS and CMS stacks, integrations, AI where it earns its place, and documentation that teams can trust for a full term.

I am a husband and a serious music fan. I'm a Christian and spend Sundays with my church community in Vancouver. Outside work I publish technical writing, follow learning technology closely, and keep studying Japanese.
