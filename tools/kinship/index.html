怎樣使用

每一步代表「目前對象的 …」。例：先點 父 表示「我的父親」，再點 父 表示「父親的父親」，再點 兄 即「祖父的兄」＝伯祖父。

  • 父 母 = 父母；兄/弟/姐/妹 = 已包含年齡。
  • 夫/妻 = 配偶；子/女 = 子女。
  • 年長/年輕 切換用於計算「堂表兄弟姊妹」時，子或女與自己的年齡關係（影響 堂兄 vs 堂弟）。
  • CE 刪除最後一步，AC 全部重置。

備註

中港臺各地的稱呼或有差異，計算機以常見漢語家族稱謂為準。如需確認，請複製名稱再查證。少數冷門關係（很深或交叉的姻親）若無對應俗稱，會以關係鏈方式顯示。

Chinese kinship calculator (English)

Chinese family titles are famously specific. There is a different word for your father's older brother (伯父) and his younger brother (叔父), and a different word again for their wives (伯母, 嬸母). Your mother's brother is 舅父, not 伯父. Your husband's father is 公公, your wife's father is 岳父. There are dozens of these and they do not translate cleanly into English's single word "uncle".

This tool composes the correct Traditional Chinese kinship title from a sequence of clicks. Each button means "… of the current person". For example, the chain father → father → older brother → son (younger) → wife resolves to 堂嬸 (tángshěn) — the wife of your paternal grandfather's older brother's younger son.

What the buttons mean

  • 父 father · 母 mother
  • 兄 older brother · 弟 younger brother
  • 姐 older sister · 妹 younger sister
  • 夫 husband · 妻 wife
  • 子 son · 女 daughter
  • 女 / 男 sets your own gender · 年長 / 年輕 sets whether a child step is older or younger (matters when computing cousin titles like 堂兄 vs 堂弟).
  • CE removes the last step · AC resets everything.

Who this is for

Heritage learners trying to remember what to call relatives at Lunar New Year, students of Mandarin or Cantonese learning the kinship vocabulary, translators handling family documents, and anyone marrying into a Chinese family who keeps meeting cousins, aunts, and grand-uncles whose titles never quite stick.

Notes

Kinship terms differ slightly across Mainland China, Hong Kong, and Taiwan. The calculator uses common Traditional Chinese forms; please verify against local usage before formal use. Very distant or unusual in-law relationships may not have a single conventional term and will be shown as the descriptive chain instead.
