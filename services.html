Services

Two lanes I take on directly: a site that carries your brand, and a system that carries your operations. When it makes sense, everything can be handed over so you own the whole stack.

Personal or company brand websites

Custom-built sites for people and teams who need more than a template. Tailored to your personal or company brand—layout, content structure, and the small details that read as intentional. A strong fit when you are growing reach, sharpening a professional presence, or need a single place that holds your story and work. I can fully hand over code and assets on request so you are not locked in.

Ballpark for something like this site: about USD 1,399, roughly 7–14 days. Bigger scope shifts the number; we align on both before writing code.

Business system MVP or technical consulting

End-to-end MVP delivery or hands-on software architecture consulting. Custom full-stack systems with development, testing, and deployment to a scale that matches where you are today—integrated with how you already run the business, not parallel to it. When you are ready to run it in-house or with another team, I can hand over in a way that is actually runnable: repos, docs, and deployment paths included.

Custom builds are quoted after we know what “done” means—usually a short scoping pass, then either phased fixed chunks or time-and-materials when the edges are fuzzy. No single price fits every system; we agree terms before serious build time.
